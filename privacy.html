Section 01

Overview

Bullxmarkets ("we," "us," or "our") operates a subscription-based trading signals and copy-trading community platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you visit our website, subscribe to any of our plans, or participate in our Discord community.

By using any Bullxmarkets service, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services and contact us for a refund where applicable.

Short version: We collect only what we need to run our service, we never sell your data to third parties, and you can request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at the details below.

Section 02

Data We Collect

We collect information in three ways: directly from you when you register or subscribe, automatically through your use of our platform, and from third-party services necessary to operate the platform.

Category Examples Why We Collect It
Account data Name, email address, username Create and manage your account
Usage data Pages visited, features used, session duration Improve the platform and diagnose bugs
Device & network data IP address, browser type, operating system Security, fraud prevention
Communications Emails and support messages sent to us Respond to enquiries and provide support

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as government IDs, health information, or biometric data. We never request your brokerage login credentials.

Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • To create your account and insure highend security.
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, account alerts, password resets). These cannot be opted out of while your account is active.
  • To send marketing and community emails, such as signal digests or product updates. You can unsubscribe from these at any time.
  • To monitor for fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations, including responses to lawful requests from authorities.
  • To improve the platform through aggregated, anonymised analytics.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you without human review.

Section 04

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only in the limited circumstances below:

Analytics Providers

We use analytics tools to understand how visitors use our website, such as pages viewed, time on site, device type, and approximate location. This data is collected via cookies or similar technologies and is used only to improve our services.

Hosting & Infrastructure

Our website and data are hosted with third-party cloud providers who store and process information on our behalf under strict confidentiality and security obligations.

Email Service Provider

We use a transactional email provider to deliver receipts and communications. Your name and email address are shared with this provider solely for message delivery.

Legal Compliance

We may disclose personal data if required to do so by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Bullxmarkets, its members, or the public.

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to equivalent privacy protections. We will notify you before such a transfer takes effect.

Section 05

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website and understand how it is used. The categories we use are:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — essential for the site to function (e.g. session management, security tokens). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand traffic patterns and popular content using aggregated, anonymised data. You can opt out via our cookie banner.
  • Preference cookies — remember settings you have chosen, such as currency display or notification preferences.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies for retargeting purposes. Third-party scripts embedded on the page (such as TradingView widgets) may set their own cookies, which are governed by those providers' policies.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time, though disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality.

Section 06

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

  • Active accounts: data is retained for the lifetime of your account.
  • Closed accounts: account data is deleted or anonymised within 90 days of account closure, except where retention is required for legal or tax compliance.
  • Payment records: billing records are retained for 7 years to satisfy financial regulations.
  • Support communications: kept for 2 years from the date of last contact.
  • Security logs: retained for up to 12 months for fraud detection purposes.
Section 07

Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement a range of technical and organisational measures including:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit between your browser and our servers.
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive fields at rest.
  • Access controls that limit personal data access to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regular security reviews and penetration testing.
  • Payment card data is never stored on our infrastructure — all card processing is delegated to Stripe.

Please note: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you suspect unauthorised access to your account, contact us immediately at the address below.

Section 08

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest, including direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the details in Section 11. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

Section 09

Minors

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

Trading in financial markets carries significant risk and is not appropriate for minors. We reserve the right to terminate any account we believe belongs to a person under 18.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal obligations. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Send a notification email to all active members at least 14 days before the changes take effect.
  • Post an announcement in our Discord server.

Your continued use of our services after the effective date of any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. If you do not agree with the changes, you may cancel your membership before they take effect.
